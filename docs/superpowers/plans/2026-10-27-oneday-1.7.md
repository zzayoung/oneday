- New library name: 记录; category default: 待办; submit label: 添加.

## Review focus

- A delayed first save must not overwrite a newer drag; failed saves must restore a coherent confirmed state.
- Undo during pending deletion must restore immediately, and running-task logs must survive trashing.
- Coverflow swipe, vertical drag, editor selection and detail tab swipe must not steal each other's gestures.
- Completion counts must not count future plans or today-state completion as historical completion without evidence.
- Drafts and attachments must survive category changes, Day selection changes and closing.
